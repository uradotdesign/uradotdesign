# Ura Design CMS

## Register

product

## Users

Ura staff and contractors edit English and German website content in Directus.
Visitors read Ura's services, work and writing, and can contact the team.

## Product Purpose

Make routine publishing and shared-content editing understandable and consistent.
The approved CMS form-unification specification defines the existing form sections;
the localization specification requires native translations and resilient German copy.

## Brand Personality

The public site's current Ura identity is the visual reference. Preserve its
typography, colors, layout and scroll glass effect. The CMS uses Directus's native,
familiar controls and clear editorial language.

## Anti-references

Avoid arbitrary layout builders, duplicate language fields, unexplained technical
controls, and differently organized forms for equivalent content. Do not redesign
the public site as part of maintenance.

## Design Principles

- Edit, reorder and show or hide content within the current design, as requested.
- Keep publishing, translated content, media, links, display and SEO predictable.
- Enforce privileges in the API; hiding a control is not access control.
- Make configuration reproducible and preserve existing editorial content.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Support English and German, keyboard navigation, visible focus, understandable
labels and errors, reduced motion, and narrow screens. Verify actual behavior
without claiming a comprehensive WCAG certification.
